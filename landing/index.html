How it works

From install to answer in under a minute.

i

Install the extension

Add ForgetMeNot to Chrome in one click. It sits quietly in your toolbar until you need it.

ii

Connect your apps

Sign in with Google to connect Gmail and Drive. Add Slack and Notion when you're ready — a one-time, 30-second setup.

iii

Ask anything

Type or speak in plain language. "Emails from David about the Q3 launch" simply works — no filters, no operators.

iv

Get answers, not links

ForgetMeNot reads across your results and gives you a written answer — with the sources it drew from, one tap away.
