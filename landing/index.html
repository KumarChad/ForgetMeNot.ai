How it works

1

Install the Chrome extension

Add ForgetMeNot to Chrome in one click. It sits quietly in your toolbar until you need it.

2

Connect your apps

Sign in with Google to connect Gmail and Drive. Add Slack and Notion when you're ready. One-time setup, takes 30 seconds.

3

Ask anything

Type or speak your query. ForgetMeNot's AI understands natural language — "emails from David about the Q3 launch" just works.

4

Get answers, not just links

ForgetMeNot doesn't just list results — it reads them and gives you an AI summary so you get the answer without opening ten tabs.
